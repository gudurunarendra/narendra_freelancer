FAQ’s

Quick answers to common questions.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Most sites see early movement in 2–3 months, and strong results usually build over 4–6 months, depending on competition and site health.

What is included in an SEO audit report?

Technical, on-page and content checks, keyword gaps, backlink review, and a prioritised action plan.

What are AEO and GEO?

Answer Engine and Generative Engine Optimization help your brand appear in AI answers such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews.

Do you provide monthly reports?

Yes. Every month you get rankings, traffic, leads, work completed and the plan for next month.

Are the backlinks safe?

Yes. I build only relevant, white-hat backlinks from quality websites.

Can you work with international or local businesses?

Yes. I handle Local SEO and International SEO for single-location and multi-country websites.
